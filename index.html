Methodology & assumptions

This tool follows the screening logic of the NYC CEQR Technical Manual, Ch. 8 (Shadows):

  • Representative days: Mar 21, May 6/Aug 6, Jun 21, Dec 21.
  • Analysis window: 1.5 hrs after sunrise to 1.5 hrs before sunset.
  • Incremental shadow: proposed shadow minus existing/as-of-right shadow, computed at each time step.
  • Area metrics: grid-sampled polygon coverage; approximate, screening-level only.

Geometry: flat-topped extrusions; ground shadow = convex hull of footprint ∪ projected top. Sun position from SunCalc. Resource polygons from OpenStreetMap (OSM/ODbL).

Limits: screening-level only. Does not model terrain, massing setbacks, or tree canopy. For a defensible EIS, use 3-D CAD/GIS shadow modeling. Concern levels are screening indicators, not CEQR significance determinations.
