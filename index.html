Methodology & assumptions

This tool follows the screening logic of the NYC CEQR Technical Manual, Ch. 8 (Shadows):

  • Representative days: Mar 21, May 6/Aug 6, Jun 21, and Dec 21.
  • Analysis window: 1.5 hours after sunrise to 1.5 hours before sunset.
  • Incremental shadow: new shadow cast by the proposed structure on sun-sensitive resources, reported as duration.

Geometry: the building is treated as a flat-topped extrusion. The ground shadow is the convex hull of the footprint and its sun-projected top (shadow length = height ÷ tan(sun altitude)). Sun position from SunCalc.

Limits: screening-level only. It models the proposed building in isolation — it does not subtract shadow already cast by existing buildings, nor model terrain or your structure’s own massing setbacks. Resource coordinates are approximate. For a defensible EIS, use 3-D massing in a CAD/GIS shadow model.
